Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if the internet goes down while ringing a customer?
The POS engine features an automatic offline queue. Transactions will continue to be processed locally and will seamlessly synchronize with the central cloud database as soon as connectivity resumes.
Why can't a Store Manager see the Monthly Sales Target in Settings?
Monthly Sales Targets are reserved strictly for the CEO and Administrator profiles to ensure centralized strategic corporate quota governance across branches.
How are Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) calculated?
Every product has an individual unit cost price configured in Inventory. When a sale completes, the system calculates actual COGS based on that unit cost rather than an estimate, giving exact gross margin accuracy in the Profitability dashboard.
How do I reset my account password if I'm locked out?
Visit the Reset Password page, input your company email, and submit. If your account is verified, you will receive reset instructions. Alternatively, request your system administrator to update your credentials.
