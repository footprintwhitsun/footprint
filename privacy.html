Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Effective Date: September 2026 • Version: 3.2 Enterprise Standard • Footprint Retail Ltd.

1. Commitment to Enterprise Privacy

Footprint ("we", "our", "the Enterprise") operates point-of-sale systems, multi-branch inventory databases, customer credit ledgers, and executive business intelligence portals. We are strictly committed to safeguarding business data, staff records, and customer personal information in full compliance with the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and international enterprise information security standards.

Zero-Trust Architecture & Cryptographic Isolation

All sensitive authentication tokens and database transactions are protected with TLS 1.3 in-transit and AES-256 at rest, guaranteeing full tenant separation and tamper-proof audit trails.

2. Categories of Information Collected

In operating our retail network, we collect and process the following operational records:

  • Transaction & Payment Data: Invoice amounts, payment tender types (Cash, MTN/Telecel Mobile Money, Credit Card, Quick Credit balances), timestamp, and cashier identifier.
  • Customer Profile Information: Customer name, contact telephone number, business TIN, delivery address, and credit account limits.
  • Staff & Operator Telemetry: User accounts, role permissions (CEO, Admin, Manager, Cashier), shift open/close balances, and system action logs.
  • Inventory & Branch Movements: Stock unit costs, selling prices, physical inventory count reconciliations, and inter-branch transfer orders.

3. Use & Processing of Collected Data

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate corporate commerce operations, including:

  • Authorizing POS register checkout transactions and generating printed or electronic sales receipts.
  • Synchronizing live inventory quantities between Accra Main, Dzorwulu, and central logistics warehouses.
  • Calculating accurate Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), VAT returns, and executive gross profit margins.
  • Preventing internal fraud and discrepancies through immutable system audit trails.

4. Data Retention & Fiscal Compliance

Sales transaction records and fiscal tax invoices are maintained for a minimum of 6 years in accordance with Ghana Revenue Authority (GRA) financial reporting regulations. Operational session caches and temporary offline queues are cleared following verified database synchronization.

5. Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC)

Access to sensitive business telemetry is strictly segmented by verified user credentials:

  • Cashiers: Restricted solely to POS ringing, cash handling, and customer receipts. Access to cost prices, profit margins, and targets is completely blocked.
  • Store Managers: Permitted to oversee branch stock, supplier purchase orders, and daily shift balancing. Monthly sales quotas and executive financial forecast configurations remain restricted to CEO/Admin.
  • CEO & Corporate Leadership: Full strategic oversight across all branches with multi-location financial consolidation.
